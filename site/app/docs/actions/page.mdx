# Actions

<Lead>Choose what a swipe reveals and what a full swipe does.</Lead>

Pick a `side`, turn `commit` on or off, and swipe the row.

<ActionsExample />

## How it works

A row can have `Sidle.Actions` on either side, or both. Swipe partway and the row opens to show them. Tapping an action runs its `onSelect` and closes the row.

```tsx
<Sidle.Actions side="end">
	<Sidle.Action onSelect={flag}>Flag</Sidle.Action>
</Sidle.Actions>
```

Without a `commit` action, that's as far as the row goes.

## Commit actions

Mark an action `commit` and a full swipe runs it. Past 60% of the row's width it fills the row, and letting go slides the row away and collapses it. A fast flick commits sooner, once it's past the panel. Tapping the action does the same.

```tsx
<Sidle.Action onSelect={remove} commit>
	Delete
</Sidle.Action>
```

Tune how much a flick helps with `commitMomentum`.

```tsx
<Sidle.Row commitMomentum={0.1}>{/* ... */}</Sidle.Row>
```

`onSelect` fires after the collapse, so it's the right moment to take the row out of your list. A commit can't be undone by the row: if `onSelect` doesn't remove it, it stays collapsed at zero height.

With more than one `commit` action on a side, a full swipe runs the last one.

## Width

A panel opens to 75px per action. Set `width` when your labels need more or less, and the actions share it evenly.

```tsx
<Sidle.Actions side="end" width={150}>{/* ... */}</Sidle.Actions>
```
